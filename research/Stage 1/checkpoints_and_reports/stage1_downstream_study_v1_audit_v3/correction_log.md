## Executive Summary of Audit v3 Corrections

This third-phase audit was conducted to repair specific remaining defects identified in the downstream added-value study reporting and implementation:
1. **Report-to-Data Reconciliation:** Discrepancy between manual markdown tables and underlying CSVs (e.g., pure_vegetarian BioNutriGPT at threshold 0.50 listed as 94 accepted instead of verified 95) resolved by programmatically generating 100% of report tables directly from verified machine-readable CSVs.
2. **Confidence-Interval Characterization:** Replaced incorrect claim that four cell comparisons exclude zero. Clarified that **0 of 45 cell-specific direct model-vs-model feasibility contrasts exclude zero** (all 45 intervals contain zero). In contrast, **12 of 45 model-vs-rules-only contrasts exclude zero** (all pure_vegetarian at thresholds 0.05–0.30, where model feasibility was strictly lower than rules-only). Reaffirmed that confidence intervals containing zero indicate insufficient evidence of difference, not proof of equivalence.
3. **Real Runner Resume Safeguards:** Replaced standalone mock safeguard tests with complete canonical SHA-256 fingerprinting, atomic writes, and failure preservation in `run_stage1_downstream_study_v2.py`. Tested the actual worker against 9 distinct failure and resume scenarios (`test_real_runner_safeguards.py`), with all 9 passing.
4. **Failure Diagnostics and Timing Breakdown:** Disaggregated 182 solver-certified infeasibilities (Status 8, solver code 2) from 4 computational timeouts (Status 13, solver code 1). Proved that 33 infeasibilities exceeded 40 ms solver execution time (mean 128.57 ms, max 965.96 ms; wall time median 33.02 ms, 44 cases $>40$ ms), correcting the prior false claim that all infeasibilities were $<40$ ms. Removed unsupported claims regarding "dual simplex presolve" and specific conflicting constraint attribution without IIS diagnostics. Documented that 4 rules-only cases also failed with infeasibility in pure_vegetarian mode (cases 864, 2640, 3408, 3984).
5. **Worker Concurrency Provenance:** Documented the discrepancy between `protocol.json` (8 workers) and execution runtime (10 workers). Removed the unsupported assertion of "zero numerical impact", acknowledging that process contention effects on 3.0-second timeouts remain unmeasured without controlled multi-core benchmarking.
6. **Removal of Overclaims:** Stripped clinical trial terminology ("intention-to-treat"), causal claims ("unbiased", "clinically meaningful"), and future certainty claims ("100% [100%, 100%]"). Documented FDA Daily Values as descriptive normalization scales to avoid near-zero division rather than individualized RDAs or Indian clinical targets. Reaffirmed that basket shifts do not prove nutritional improvement.

All 4,800 saved cases, source code hashes, and participant records remain preserved and verified. No optimizer solves were rerun.
