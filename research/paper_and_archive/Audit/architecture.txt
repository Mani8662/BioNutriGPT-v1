1. ARCHITECTURAL OVERVIEW & HIGH-LEVEL INFORMATION FLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BioNutriGPT Stage 1 is a multimodal tabular neural network designed to predict
three chronic cardiometabolic disease risks (Type 2 Diabetes, Obesity, and
Cardiovascular Disease) from 88 preprocessed clinical features across four
distinct biological streams.

The architecture addresses two fundamental flaws in traditional tabular healthcare models:
  1. Feature Signal Drowning: Traditional multi-layer perceptrons (MLPs) and naive
     transformers flatten all features into one vector, allowing 46 dense nutrition
     columns to drown out 6 critical blood biomarkers (e.g., HbA1c).
  2. The Early-Pooling Bottleneck: Multi-tower architectures compress modalities
     separately before fusion, mathematically preventing individual nutrient tokens
     from interacting directly with individual blood biomarkers.

BioNutriGPT Stage 1 solves this through a 5-step hierarchical design:
```
  [Input Features: 88 Preprocessed Tabular Values]
                        │
                        ▼
  [Step 1: Feature Tokenizer (FT-Transformer Style)]
  - Independent Linear Projections (W_j * x_j + b_j) -> 88 Tokens [B, 88, 128]
  - Additive Modality Type Embeddings (M_k) -> Bio, Diet, Life, Demo
                        │
                        ▼
  [Step 2: Cross-Modal Transformer Backbone]
  - 3-Layer Pre-Norm Transformer Encoder (nhead=4, d_model=128, dim_ff=256)
  - Full Cross-Attention: HbA1c directly attends to Sugars, Fiber & Sitting Time
                        │
                        ▼
  [Step 3: Modality-Specific Attention Pooling]
  - Query-Projected Softmax Attention on each modality slice
  - Compresses 88 contextual tokens into 4 pure vectors: z_bio, z_diet, z_life, z_demo
                        │
                        ▼
  [Step 4: Sparse Adaptive Mixture of Experts (MoE)]
  - Concatenated Vector u = [z_bio || z_diet || z_life || z_demo] (512-d)
  - Top-2 Softmax Gating across 4 Expert MLPs + Load-Balancing Regularization
  - Outputs Fused Patient Phenotypic State: z_patient (256-d)
                        │
                        ▼
  [Step 5: Multi-Task Clinical Residual Skip Heads]
  - Diabetes Head : [z_patient || z_bio] (384-d) -> Linear -> P(Diabetes)
  - CVD Head      : [z_patient || z_bio] (384-d) -> Linear -> P(CVD)
  - Obesity Head  : [z_patient || z_diet || z_life] (512-d) -> Linear -> P(Obesity)
```

2. INPUT LAYER & STRICT ANTI-LEAKAGE PARTITIONS (88 FEATURES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The model takes an input tensor x in R^{B x 88}. The 88 features are divided into
four distinct physiological streams:

Stream 1: Blood Biomarkers (6 features) [Indices 0 to 5]
  - Features: LBXGH (HbA1c), LBDHDD (HDL Cholesterol), LBXTC (Total Cholesterol),
              NON_HDL_CHOL (Derived Non-HDL), CHOL_HDL_RATIO (Total/HDL ratio),
              NON_HDL_TO_HDL_RATIO.
  - Clinical Rationale: Universal non-fasting lipid and glycemic markers capturing
    3-month blood glucose memory and total atherogenic particle burden.

Stream 2: 24-Hour Nutrition & Dietary Ratios (46 features) [Indices 6 to 51]
  - 39 Daily Nutrients: Calories, Protein, Carbohydrates, Total Sugars, Dietary Fiber,
    Total Fat, Saturated Fat, Monounsaturated Fat, Polyunsaturated Fat, Cholesterol,
    Vitamins (A, B1, B2, B6, B12, C, E, K, Folate), Minerals (Calcium, Phosphorus,
    Magnesium, Iron, Zinc, Copper, Sodium, Potassium, Selenium), Caffeine, Alcohol.
  - 7 Clinical Interaction Ratios: % calories from Protein, Carbs, and Fat;
    Fiber density per 1,000 kcal; Sugar-to-Fiber ratio; Sodium-to-Potassium ratio;
    Saturated-to-Unsaturated Fat ratio.

Stream 3: Lifestyle, Physical Activity & Sleep (19 features) [Indices 52 to 70]
  - 9 Continuous Variables: Sleep Hours, Sleep Deviation (|Sleep - 7.5h|), Daily
    Sitting Minutes (PAD680), 5 WHO GPAQ Activity Domains (Vigorous Work, Moderate
    Work, Walking/Biking Transport, Vigorous Gym, Moderate Gym), Total Weekly METs.
  - 6 Binary Flags: Physician-diagnosed sleep disorder (SLQ050), and 5 GPAQ Yes/No
    screening responses (PAQ605, PAQ620, PAQ635, PAQ650, PAQ665).
  - 4 One-Hot Smoking Categories: Never, Former, Some Days, Every Day.

Stream 4: Demographics & Missingness Indicators (17 features) [Indices 71 to 87]
  - 3 Continuous/Ordinal: Age (RIDAGEYR), Poverty-Income Ratio (INDFMPIR), Education (DMDEDUC2).
  - 9 Binary/One-Hot: Female flag (SEX_FEMALE), Race/Ethnicity (5 categories),
    Marital Status (3 categories).
  - 5 Informative Missingness Flags: INDFMPIR_MISS, SLEEP_HOURS_MISS, PAD680_MISS,
    PA_TOTAL_MET_MIN_WK_MISS, OTHER_FEATURE_MISS.

CRITICAL ANTI-LEAKAGE DESIGN NOTE:
  - Anthropometric variables (BMXBMI / Body Mass Index, BMXWT / Weight, BMXHT / Height)
    are strictly FORBIDDEN and EXCLUDED from Stage 1 inputs.
  - Because TARGET_OBESITY is clinically defined as BMI >= 30.0, including BMI or weight
    causes 100% circular target leakage. The network is forced to learn true latent
    metabolic, nutritional, and physical activity drivers of obesity.
  - SEQN (Patient ID) and NHANES_CYCLE (Survey Wave) are preserved as metadata only.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. STEP 1: PER-FEATURE TOKENIZER (FT-TRANSFORMER STYLE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mathematical Formulation:
  - Let d_model = 128.
  - Traditional models project the entire vector at once via Linear(88, 128). This
    collapses individual feature identities.
  - In BioNutriGPT Stage 1, each feature j in {1, ..., 88} has its own dedicated
    learnable projection weight W_j in R^{128} and bias b_j in R^{128}:
        e_j = x_j * W_j + b_j  in R^{128}
  - In PyTorch tensor notation, this is vectorized across the batch B:
        tokens = (x.unsqueeze(-1) * self.weight.unsqueeze(0)) + self.bias.unsqueeze(0)
        tokens in R^{B x 88 x 128}

Modality Type Embeddings:
  - To prevent 46 nutrition columns from statistically overwhelming the 6 blood tests,
    a learnable modality embedding table M in R^{4 x 128} is maintained:
        M_bio  for features 0..5
        M_diet for features 6..51
        M_life for features 52..70
        M_demo for features 71..87
  - Final Input Tokens to Transformer:
        E_j = e_j + M_{modality(j)}  in R^{B x 88 x 128}

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. STEP 2: CROSS-MODAL TRANSFORMER BACKBONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Architecture:
  - 3 stacked Pre-LayerNorm Transformer Encoder Layers (nn.TransformerEncoder).
  - Configuration:
      * Embedding Dimension (d_model)   : 128
      * Multi-Head Attention Heads      : 4 (head_dim = 32 per head)
      * Feedforward Expansion (dim_ff)  : 256
      * Activation Function             : GELU (Gaussian Error Linear Unit)
      * Dropout Rate                    : 0.10
      * Normalization Architecture      : Pre-Norm (norm_first = True) for gradient stability.

Biological Mechanism & Function:
  - Unlike multi-tower models that isolate data streams, this full 88 x 88 attention
    matrix allows fine-grained, bidirectional biological cross-talk:
      * HbA1c tokens attend directly to Total Sugar and Dietary Fiber tokens.
      * Non-HDL Cholesterol tokens attend directly to Saturated Fat and Sitting Minutes.
  - Output: Contextualized token representations H in R^{B x 88 x 128}.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. STEP 3: MODALITY-SPECIFIC ATTENTION POOLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mathematical Formulation:
  - Global average pooling (H.mean(dim=1)) naively assigns equal weight (1/88) to all
    tokens, diluting critical clinical signals.
  - We partition the contextualized tokens H back into their 4 modality subspaces:
      H_bio  in R^{B x 6 x 128}
      H_diet in R^{B x 46 x 128}
      H_life in R^{B x 19 x 128}
      H_demo in R^{B x 17 x 128}
  - For each modality subspace m in {bio, diet, life, demo} with K_m tokens, the model
    learns an independent attention query projection vector v_m in R^{128}:
        score_{m, k} = (H_{m, k} . v_m) / sqrt(128)
        a_{m, k} = exp(score_{m, k}) / sum_{i=1}^{K_m} exp(score_{m, i})
  - Pooled Modality Representation:
        z_m = sum_{k=1}^{K_m} a_{m, k} * H_{m, k}  in R^{B x 128}

Clinical Advantage & Explainability:
  - The learned attention weights a_{m, k} directly quantify feature importance per patient.
  - In practice, HbA1c (LBXGH) and Non-HDL (NON_HDL_CHOL) capture over 35% of the total
    biomarker attention weight, confirming that the network actively prioritizes
    clinically actionable biomarkers over baseline demographic noise.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. STEP 4: SPARSE ADAPTIVE MIXTURE OF EXPERTS (MoE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sub-Module Architecture:
  - Input Concatenation:
        u = [z_bio || z_diet || z_life || z_demo] in R^{B x 512}
  - Expert Sub-Networks:
    Four specialized feedforward expert MLPs (E_1, E_2, E_3, E_4).
    Each expert architecture:
        Linear(512, 256) -> LayerNorm(256) -> GELU() -> Dropout(0.1) -> Linear(256, 256)
  - Top-2 Gating Network:
    A linear router Linear(512, 4) produces routing logits s(u) in R^{B x 4}.
    The network identifies the top two expert indices:
        top2_indices = TopK(s(u), k=2)
    Gating weights g_{k1}, g_{k2} are computed via Softmax over those two logits only.
  - Fused Patient State:
        z_patient = sum_{k in Top2} g_k(u) * E_k(u)  in R^{B x 256}

Load-Balancing Auxiliary Loss (Switch Transformer Formulation):
  - To prevent "expert collapse" (where one expert handles 100% of patients while others
    remain untrained), an auxiliary entropy regularization term is calculated:
        importance_i = (1/B) * sum_{b=1}^B Softmax(s_b)_i
        load_i       = (1/B) * sum_{b=1}^B I(i in top2_indices_b)
        L_balance    = 4.0 * sum_{i=1}^4 (importance_i * load_i)
  - Added to the total objective with coefficient lambda_aux = 0.01.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. STEP 5: CLINICAL RESIDUAL SKIP HIGHWAYS & MULTI-TASK PREDICTION HEADS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Clinical Residual Skip Design:
  - Passing through deep layers can cause subtle blood test sensitivities to decay.
  - To ensure absolute clinical sensitivity, we inject direct residual skip highways
    from specific pooled modalities into task-specific heads:

Head 1: Type 2 Diabetes Head
  - Concatenation: [z_patient (256-d) || z_bio (128-d)] in R^{B x 384}
  - Layers: Linear(384, 64) -> GELU() -> Dropout(0.1) -> Linear(64, 1)
  - Output: logit_diabetes in R^B (Sigmoid yields P(Diabetes))
  - Medical Rationale: Direct injection of z_bio ensures HbA1c and lipid ratios
    maintain immediate mathematical control over diabetic predictions.

Head 2: Cardiovascular Disease (CVD) Head
  - Concatenation: [z_patient (256-d) || z_bio (128-d)] in R^{B x 384}
  - Layers: Linear(384, 64) -> GELU() -> Dropout(0.1) -> Linear(64, 1)
  - Output: logit_cvd in R^B (Sigmoid yields P(CVD))
  - Medical Rationale: Guarantees circulating atherogenic non-HDL and total/HDL ratios
    directly drive coronary and vascular risk scores.

Head 3: Metabolic Obesity Head
  - Concatenation: [z_patient (256-d) || z_diet (128-d) || z_life (128-d)] in R^{B x 512}
  - Layers: Linear(512, 64) -> GELU() -> Dropout(0.1) -> Linear(64, 1)
  - Output: logit_obesity in R^B (Sigmoid yields P(Obesity))
  - Medical Rationale: Since physical body weight is excluded to prevent leakage,
    obesity prediction is directly driven by energy intake (z_diet), sedentary sitting,
    and physical activity domains (z_life).

9. WHAT IS STAGE 2 IN SIMPLE TERMS?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If Stage 1 is the "Diagnostic Specialist" that identifies what health risks a 
person faces (Type 2 Diabetes, Obesity, or Cardiovascular Disease), Stage 2 is the 
"Precision Clinical Nutritionist."

Stage 2 answers one specific, life-saving question:
  "Given this patient's unique body measurements, blood tests, and disease risks, 
   what exact daily nutrient numbers (calories, proteins, fats, carbs, vitamins, 
   and minerals) will safely lower their disease risk without starving them or 
   causing nutritional deficiencies?"

Instead of handing out generic, printed diet brochures ("eat less sugar, eat more 
salad"), Stage 2 computes a mathematically certified, personalized nutrient 
prescription tailored to that exact individual.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. HOW DOES STAGE 2 TAKE ITS INPUTS?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stage 2 ingests two streams of information:

1. The Patient's Current Baseline (From Stage 1):
   - The 88 clinical features: Current blood markers (HbA1c, Cholesterol), 
     current lifestyle habits (sitting time, sleep), and their current 24-hour 
     food intake (what they normally eat).
   - Stage 1's disease risk probabilities: Exactly how high their risk is for 
     Diabetes, Obesity, and Heart Disease.

2. The Patient's Physical Body Measurements (Clinical Anthropometric Metadata):
   - Height (cm), Weight (kg), Age, Sex, and Physical Activity Level.
   
Why is this separated from Stage 1?
- In Stage 1, height and weight were strictly hidden so the model couldn't "cheat" 
  on diagnosing obesity (zero circular leakage).
- But in Stage 2, your real height, weight, and age are essential! A 115 kg woman 
  needs a completely different calorie and protein target than a 58 kg athlete. 
  Stage 2 uses these physical measurements to calculate the person's true metabolic 
  needs using the medical Mifflin-St Jeor equation.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. WHAT HAPPENS INSIDE STAGE 2? (THE OPTIMIZATION PROCESS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stage 2 works through three clear, sequential steps:

Step A: Calculating the Personal Energy Budget
- It calculates the patient's Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)—the minimum calories their 
  body burns just staying alive.
- It then calculates their Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) based on their 
  daily activity.
- If the patient is overweight or high risk, it establishes a safe, medically sound 
  caloric deficit (typically 500 kcal/day). It NEVER starves the patient below their 
  vital metabolic baseline.

Step B: Counterfactual Optimization (The "Smart Tweak")
- In computer science, a "counterfactual" means: "What would happen if we change X?"
- Stage 2 takes the 39 nutrients the patient normally eats and uses a mathematical 
  optimizer (Projected Gradient Descent via SLSQP / L-BFGS-B).
- It asks the Stage 1 model: "If we nudge sugar down, increase fiber, reduce 
  saturated fat, or adjust sodium, how much does the patient's heart and diabetes 
  risk drop?"
- At the same time, it applies a "Smooth Quadratic Barrier Penalty." This prevents 
  the AI from recommending wild, impossible changes (like telling someone to eat 
  zero fat or 500 grams of fiber overnight). It keeps the new diet as close to 
  normal, palatable eating habits as possible while maximizing health benefits.

Step C: Producing the Candidate Target
- The optimizer outputs a recommended daily target for all 39 nutrients:
  Total Calories, Protein, Carbohydrates, Total Sugars, Dietary Fiber, 
  Total Fat, Saturated Fat, Monounsaturated Fat, Polyunsaturated Fat, 
  Cholesterol, Sodium, Potassium, and 24 essential vitamins and minerals.

12. HOW IS IT VALIDATED? (THE 4 STRICT SAFETY FILTERS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stage 2 does not just produce numbers and stop. Every single output must pass 
through four consecutive clinical "safety gates" (Filters). If any number violates 
medical safety or the laws of physics, it is immediately flagged and corrected:

* FILTER 1: THE LAW OF CONSERVATION OF ENERGY (Atwater Thermodynamic Balance)
  - In physics, energy cannot appear from nowhere. In nutrition science:
      Total Calories = (4 x Carbs) + (4 x Protein) + (9 x Fat) + (7 x Alcohol)
  - Mathematical optimizers can sometimes have minor rounding discrepancies (e.g. 
    a 3% gap).
  - Filter 1 automatically recalibrates the exact grams of carbohydrates and fats 
    so that the energy matches the calorie target with an EXACT 0.00% error. 
    Every single calorie is accounted for.

* FILTER 2: MEDICAL SAFETY CLAMPS (Institute of Medicine Safe Envelopes)
  - The engine checks every single nutrient against the official National Academy 
    of Medicine / Institute of Medicine (IOM) Dietary Reference Intakes (DRI).
  - Recommended Dietary Allowance (RDA): Ensures the person gets enough vitamins 
    and minerals to prevent deficiencies (Iron, Calcium, Vitamin D, B12, etc.).
  - Tolerable Upper Intake Level (UL): Ensures no nutrient exceeds toxic maximums.
  - Safe Sodium Clamp: Sodium is strictly capped at 2,300 mg/day (or 1,500 mg/day 
    for cardiovascular patients as per American Heart Association guidelines).
  - Safe Potassium Clamp: Minimum 2,600 mg/day to protect kidneys and arteries.
  - Safe Protein Scaling: Protein is strictly clamped to between 0.80 g/kg and 
    2.0 g/kg of the person's actual body weight, protecting kidneys while preserving 
    lean muscle mass.

* FILTER 3: CLINICAL RATIO COHERENCE (Heart & Metabolic Balance)
  - Individual nutrients don't work in isolation; their ratios matter for disease. 
    Filter 3 verifies three crucial health ratios:
    1. Sugar-to-Fiber Ratio: Must drop to prevent glucose and insulin spikes.
    2. Sodium-to-Potassium Ratio: Must stay below 1.0 to keep blood pressure healthy.
    3. Saturated-to-Unsaturated Fat Ratio: Must stay below 0.40 or improve over baseline.
  - Auto-Remediation: If saturated fat is slightly high, Filter 3 automatically 
    clamps it down to satisfy cardiovascular safety rules.

* FILTER 4: CLOSED-LOOP MODEL RESCORING (Real-World Verification)
  - Stage 2 takes the newly prescribed diet and feeds it back into the Stage 1 
    diagnostic neural network along with the patient's blood tests.
  - It mathematically verifies that the new diet causes disease probabilities 
    to decrease (Delta P <= 0) and does not accidentally increase any other disease.

13. WHY CAN IT BE TRUSTED? (HOW WE KNOW IT IS NOT HALLUCINATING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Hallucination" happens when AI chatbots (like standard LLMs) guess text that sounds 
convincing but has no factual basis.

Stage 2 CANNOT hallucinate numbers because of three architectural safeguards:

1. It is a Deterministic Mathematical Solver, NOT a Text Chatbot:
   - Stage 2 is built using rigorous constrained numerical optimization (SLSQP), 
     scikit-learn scalers, and PyTorch tensor gradients.
   - It is not "writing" numbers out of memory; it is calculating minimum-risk 
     points on a multidimensional mathematical curve.

2. Hard Physical & Medical Boundaries (The Unbreakable Digital Cage):
   - The safety filters (IOM tables, Atwater balance, and ratio clamps) act as 
     unbreakable boundaries.
   - Even if an algorithm tried to recommend 5,000 calories, 0 grams of protein, 
     or 10,000 mg of sodium, Filter 1 and Filter 2 would immediately clamp those 
     values back into the medically certified safe range.

3. Verified Personalized Responsiveness (Not Generic):
   - When audited across different patient archetypes:
     * For a Diabetic: It cuts sugar by 77% (down to 25 g) and increases fiber 
       by 191% (up to 35 g).
     * For a Heart Disease Patient: It cuts saturated fat by 72% and clamps sodium 
       to 1,500 mg, while boosting potassium.
     * For Severe Obesity: It safely cuts 1,358 excess calories while keeping protein 
       high (95 g) to protect muscle.
     * For a Lean Athlete: It maintains normal calories and electrolytes without 
       unnecessary restrictions.
   - This proves the engine responds to the individual, not a generic template.

14. UNDERSTANDING FILTER 4 ON CHRONIC DISEASES (CLINICAL REALISM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When tested on patients with severe established chronic disease (e.g. someone who 
has had diabetes for 10 years with an HbA1c of 7.6%), Stage 2 substantially lowers 
their heart disease risk (-24.0%) and obesity risk (-8.8%).

However, human blood tests (like HbA1c) measure 90 days of red blood cell history. 
A single 24-hour diet change cannot physically wipe out high blood HbA1c overnight. 
Because their blood markers remain elevated, Stage 1 still identifies them as diabetic.

Filter 4 strictly requires that ALL THREE disease risks must decrease simultaneously 
to grant a "Stage 3 Certificate."
- On lifestyle-driven cases, all 4 filters pass with certified_for_stage3: True.
- On severe chronic cases, Filter 4 honestly signals that nutrition must work 
  alongside physician-guided medical therapy, refusing to make unrealistic claims 
  of instant, overnight disease cures.

16. STAGE 3 ARCHITECTURAL SPECIFICATION: DISCRETE OPERATIONS RESEARCH (MILP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. The Bridge Between Biochemistry & Real-World Food:
   - Stage 2 outputs certified continuous targets for 39 macro- and micronutrients.
   - However, humans cannot eat continuous grams of isolated chemical compounds. 
     Stage 3 solves the combinatorial translation from continuous nutrient bounds 
     into integer servings of real, culturally authentic cooked Indian meals.

2. Cultural Recipe Matrix (ICMR-NIN IFCT 2017 + RecipeDB):
   - Decoupled Database Matrix: F in R^(39 x 528), representing 528 verified Indian 
     foods and composite cooked dishes analyzed across 151 chemical components.
   - Standard Cooked Household Portions: Quantized as discrete integer variables:
       q_i in {0, 1, 2, 3} (e.g., 2 Katoris Dal, 3 Phulkas, 1 Cup Sabzi).
   - Local-First Caching: Deployed via an in-memory Parquet matrix (<5 ms load time), 
     completely eliminating live API latency lag and external server outages.

3. Mathematical Formulation (Adaptive Goal Programming with L1 Slacks):
   - Objective Function:
       min ||(F * q)_kcal - T_kcal||_1 + sum_k w_k * (eps_k^+ + eps_k^-) + gamma * sum_i c_i * z_i
   - Linear Boundary Constraints:
       L_k - eps_k^- <= sum_i F_{k,i} * q_i <= U_k + eps_k^+
   - Binary Meal Selection Logic:
       q_i <= M_max * z_i,  where z_i in {0, 1}
       S_min <= sum_i z_i <= S_max (Meal diversity clamp: 4 to 8 unique dishes).
   - Algorithmic Safeguards:
       1. Adaptive L1 Slack Penalties: Prevents solver infeasibility when nutrient bounds conflict.
       2. Strict Time-Limit Budget: Enforces time_limit = 0.05s per patient case.
       3. Branch-and-Bound HiGHS Solver: Solves integer combinatorial optimization in <7.3 ms.

4. Empirical Benchmark Proof (17,046 Total Cases):
   - Track 1 (10,000 Synthetic Stress Cases): 99.94% pass rate, 99.56% caloric accuracy, 72.78s runtime.
   - Track 2 (7,046 Real CDC NHANES Patients): 99.99% pass rate, 99.62% caloric accuracy, 51.16s runtime.
   - Total Performance: 99.96% overall feasibility across 17,046 evaluations in 123.94s total time.

17. EXPLAINABLE AI (XAI) LAYER: RISK DECONSTRUCTION & PREVENTATIVE ACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Beyond Black-Box Probabilities (TRIPOD-AI Compliance):
   - Opaque probability numbers (e.g., "CVD 64%") are rejected by clinicians and patients 
     because they fail to explain WHY the patient is at risk and WHAT actions will reverse it.
   - BioNutriGPT implements a full-spectrum XAI layer spanning 17 clinical domains:
     * 9 Nutritional Risk Domains: Glycemic dynamics (Sugar/Fiber), vascular electrolytes 
       (Na/K), lipid quality (Saturated fat % kcal), microbiome fiber barrier, protein muscle 
       defense (g/kg), bone minerals (Ca, Mg), hematological oxygen (Fe, B12), antioxidant defense, 
       and toxicological load (alcohol).
     * 8 Weight & Lifestyle Domains: Thermodynamic energy balance (caloric delta vs. TDEE), 
       BMI adiposity, sarcopenic obesity index, sitting time (PAD680), weekly MET volume, 
       active transport, circadian sleep debt, and nocturnal sleep disturbance (SLQ050).

2. Two-Tier Staged Clinical Progression (Eliminating Alarmist Phrasing):
   - Pathology does not jump straight to catastrophic organ failure. The XAI engine provides:
     * Action to Minimize Risk: Clear, actionable dietary and lifestyle adjustments.
     * Early Manifestation (Reversible): Immediate short-term metabolic friction (e.g., postprandial 
       lethargy, muscle LPL shutdown, transient vascular tension) fully reversible in weeks.
     * Advanced Progression (Long-Term): Structural pathophysiology occurring over years if unaddressed 
       (e.g., beta-cell exhaustion, left ventricular remodeling, coronary plaque calcification).
   - Dynamic Patient Tailoring:
     * Optimal/Healthy metrics trigger: "Healthy level! Continue current lifestyle routine."
     * Borderline metrics trigger: Actionable preventative steps + early reversible symptoms only.
     * High-risk metrics trigger: Full staged clinical guidance with prioritized physician review.

3. Role of Stage 4 Local LLM (Patient Communicator & Health Educator):
   - Built on open-weights local execution (Qwen2.5-7B / Llama-3.1-8B, 4-bit quantized).
   - Functions strictly under neuro-symbolic guardrails: cannot alter food grams, nutrient sums,
