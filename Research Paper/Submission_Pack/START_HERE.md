## Package and limits

Eight editable Word drafts have PDF review copies. Four figures and an optional graphical abstract have 300 dpi PNG, SVG and vector PDF versions. Reproducibility contains code snapshots, configurations and hashes. The package contains aggregate evidence, not LASI participant records, frozen participant-level scenarios, raw clinical dialogue logs, source survey archives, private sessions or model weights. Those measured artifacts remain local and subject to source terms. Historical 29/31-entry results and the earlier LASI audit are separated under Evidence/Historical. The failed export attempt is excluded.

This is selected-case computational evidence, not Indian Stage 1 validation, national population performance, patient acceptability or clinical benefit. Author confirmations, a target journal, repaired prediction analyses, a matched current-catalog LP/MILP study, a frozen public release and final official checklists remain pending.

PDFs are exported through installed Microsoft Word and rasterized for layout review because LibreOffice is unavailable here. PACK_MANIFEST.json records file hashes; _QA is internal and omitted from the ZIP. No new scientific benchmark is executed during this document refresh.
