# Figure legends

**Figure 1 Architecture.** Four-stage workflow. Revisions return to Stage 3 for optimization and independent validation. Observed patient data remain separate from proposed and accepted plan state. The diagram does not imply clinical validation or causal improvement.

**Figure 2 Saved Stage 1 discrimination.** Unweighted AUROCs from `stage1_test_evaluation_report.json` on the 7,046-participant NHANES 2017–March 2020 temporal holdout. Values are not newly generated during packaging. No calibration, confidence interval or overall superiority is inferred from this plot.

**Figure 3 Current catalog coverage.** The current schema has 30 active planning fields, seven report-only fields and two separate exposure fields. Forty-three of 49 records have no declared active-field gaps before dietary/preference filtering. Completeness does not establish nutrient assay accuracy, clinical appropriateness or allergen safety.

**Optional graphical abstract draft.** Conceptual overview of screening, rules, optimization and controlled revision. This is a draft visual rather than an additional experiment or a claim of clinical effectiveness. Adapt its size and file format only after selecting the journal.

PNG files are exported at 300 dpi; SVG files retain editable vector elements; PDF files are vector exports. Final journal specifications may differ. Calibration curves and exhaustive semantic LLM reliability remain outside this study.

**Figure 4 Verified v4 conversational comparison.** Left: protocol passes on the matched 12-participant, 24-turn subset (24/24 guarded, 2/24 Qwen).
