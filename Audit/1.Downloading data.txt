--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHERE WE GOT THE DATA (STEPS FOLLOWED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1 - Existing Raw Survey File (2005-2018)
  - File   : P/BioNutriGPT_final_dataset_2005_2018.csv (42,143 adults, 839 columns)
  - Source : U.S. CDC NHANES survey years 2005-2006 through 2017-2018.

Step 2 - Downloaded More Recent Data (2017-March 2020 Pre-Pandemic)
  - File   : Models/Less Data/Data/NHANES_2017_2020_PrePandemic_Raw.csv (9,693 adults, 379 columns)
  - Script : Models/Less Data/Data/download_nhanes_2017_2020.py
  - Source : Downloaded 15 official CDC NHANES data files (demographics, blood labs,
             body measures, medical conditions, diabetes, food diary, exercise,
             sleep, smoking, alcohol) and matched people by Patient ID (SEQN).
  - Kept adults age 18+ (15,560 total people -> 9,693 adults; 5,867 children removed)
    and calculated Yes/No labels for Diabetes, Obesity, and Heart Disease (CVD).

Step 3 - Merged Both Raw Files Into One Cleanly (2005-2020)
  - File   : Models/Less Data/Data/BioNutriGPT_merged_raw_2005_2020.csv
             (45,980 adults, 848 columns)
  - Script : Models/Less Data/Data/merge_raw_datasets.py
  - Removed 2017-2018 Overlap: Excluded the 5,856 rows labeled "2017-2018" from
    the old 2005-2018 file before adding the 9,693 rows from 2017-2020.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. IMPORTANT POINTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Why 2017-2018 Was Excluded During Merge:
    CDC combined 2017-2018 and 2019-March 2020 into one "2017-2020 Pre-Pandemic"
    release with new Patient IDs. Dropping standalone 2017-2018 makes sure the same
    5,856 people are not counted twice.

- Why Total Raw Records = 45,980 (Adults Only):
    Each NHANES wave surveys ~10,000 people total, but ~40% are children under 18.
    Children were excluded because adult Obesity (BMI >= 30) and adult disease
    questions do not apply to children.

- Untouched Raw Values:
    No numbers were cleaned, filled, or scaled inside BioNutriGPT_merged_raw_2005_2020.csv.
    Empty cells (NaNs) and survey codes stayed completely raw for Step 2 (Cleaning).

- Dropped 2021-2023 completely so we could KEEP all 10 full 2007-2020 lifestyle
    features (work activity, walking/biking, and SLQ050 doctor sleep disorder).
