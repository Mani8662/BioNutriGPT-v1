Model Architecture (BioNutriGPTStage1 ~4.1M parameters):

   * Full 88 x 88 attention allows direct biological cross-talk (e.g. HbA1c attends directly 
     to Sugar, Fiber, and Sitting minutes).

   * Learned query vectors (v_bio, v_diet, v_life, v_demo) dynamically pool contextualized 
     tokens into 4 clean vectors in R^128. HbA1c and Non-HDL capture >35% of biomarker attention.
4. Sparse Adaptive Mixture of Experts (MoE):
   * Concatenated vector u = [z_bio || z_diet || z_life || z_demo] (512-d).
   * Top-2 gating across 4 specialized MLP experts (512 -> 256 -> 256) with Switch-Transformer 
     load-balancing auxiliary loss (lambda = 0.01).
   * Yields fused patient phenotypic state z_patient in R^256.
5. Clinical Residual Skip Highways:
   * Direct skip connections inject z_bio directly into Diabetes and CVD heads, bypassing 
     fusion to mathematically prevent subtle blood test sensitivity from decaying across deep layers.

Optimization Formulation:

    Verifies 4*Protein + 4*Carb + 9*Fat + 7*Alcohol == Total kcal with exact 0.00% math error.
  * Filter 2 (IOM DRI Safety Clamps):
    Strictly clamps all 26 micronutrients, sodium (<=1500mg for CVD; <=2300mg general),
